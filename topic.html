Our Inspiration

My inspiration for this coffee shop began in Kenya. Where I grew up surrounded by coffee plantations.
I remember seeing coffee plants growing in the rich soil and watching the journey of coffee from the farm to the cup.

This coffee shop is inspired by those memories and my connection to Kenya.
My goal is to create a welcoming place where people can enjoy great coffee while experiencing a small part of the culture and story behind every cup.
